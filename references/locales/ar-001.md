# Arabic (ar-001) translation guide

## At a glance
| | |
|---|---|
| Roblox locale / column | `ar-001` (no bare `ar` column needed: Arabic has one Roblox locale, Modern Standard Arabic for every Arab region) |
| Script, direction | Arabic, RTL; Western digits 0-9; Robux, Premium, keys and codes stay in Latin letters |
| Address the player as | masculine singular "you" (أنت، -ك), the accepted generic and what Roblox's own Arabic UI uses; prefer forms that read gender-neutral in writing; never slash forms (اضغط/ي) or plural (اضغطوا) |
| Text expansion vs English | about even overall on this guide's reference set (0.98x in characters): sentences -35% to +15%, short labels -35% to +60% ("Shop" -> "المتجر"), two-letter ones far more ("On" -> "مشغّل", 2.5x); Arabic glyphs also need more height than Latin (unverified ratio) |
| Plural categories (CLDR) | zero, one, two, few (n%100 = 3-10), many (n%100 = 11-99), other (100-102, 1000...). Ordinal: other only |
| Model confidence | medium: MSA is high-resource, but number and gender agreement around {params}, RTL ordering and dialect leakage go wrong often |
| Native review before shipping | strongly recommended, with a look at every string in Studio (RTL bugs only show on screen) |

## Audience and register
Roblox has been fully localized into Arabic, with automatic game translation, since February 11, 2025. Players are kids from Morocco to Oman who speak very different dialects at home but all read Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى) from school, dubbed cartoons and app UIs. One column serves them all, so write light, modern MSA: plain words, short sentences, no dialect.
- Light MSA, not classical: no case endings, no ornate openers (لقد، إنّ، عزيزي اللاعب), no rare literary words. Microsoft's Arabic guide asks for the conversational end of MSA: "أيضًا" not "كذلك", "التالي" not "اللاحق", "يمكنك" not "يتسنى لك".
- No dialect, ever: "تريد" not عايز / تبي / بدك; "الآن" not دلوقتي / الحين / هلق; "جيد" not كويس / زين.
- Buttons are verbal nouns (مصدر), like Roblox's own Arabic UI: شراء (Buy), إلغاء (Cancel), تأكيد (Confirm), متابعة (Continue), انضمام (Join), إغلاق (Close). Main-menu "Play" is the usual exception: العب, as in Roblox's own Arabic taglines ("العب على منصّة Roblox"). Roblox's Play button itself says تشغيل, which next to the "مشغّل" (On) toggle reads like a power switch.
- Instructions inside sentences use the masculine singular imperative, as Roblox does ("اضغط على E للفتح"). Real imperative, never "قم بـ" + noun: "افتح المتجر", not "قم بفتح المتجر".
- Gender-neutral for free where possible: past tense 2nd person is the same written word for both genders (حصلت = you got), so is the suffix -ك (عملاتك) and أنت. Present, future and imperative are not (تحصل / تحصلين، اضغط / اضغطي); use the masculine there.
- Hype is short: "رائع!"، "مذهل!"، "حظ رائع!"، "رقم قياسي جديد!". Not "تهانينا على هذا الإنجاز العظيم". One "!" at most.
- Errors stay calm and plain: "الكود غير صالح أو انتهت صلاحيته." Never blame: no "أخطأت".
- Kid-safe and culture-aware: no religious words for game mechanics (rebirth is not "البعث", which means resurrection); "حظ" (luck) is fine, gambling words (قمار، رهان) are not.

## Grammar traps in UI strings
- Plurals with {count}: Arabic number-noun agreement changes with the number (1 -> عملة واحدة، 2 -> عملتان, both written without the digit; 3-10 عملات، 11-99 عملة، 100 عملة، 103 عملات), and 0 is normally "لا توجد عملات". No single noun form survives a param. Never put the counted noun after {count}. Use a label: "العملات: {count}"; a bare number: "ينقصك {amount}"; or Roblox's own trick, the unit in parentheses: "لمدة {n} (دقيقة)".
- English plural can be a dual: "both arrows" is "السهمان", and "2 pets" in a fixed string is "حيوانان", never "2 حيوانات".
- {player} gender: an Arabic verb agrees with its subject (انضمّ / انضمّت). Put the verb first and use the masculine generic, as Roblox does: "انضم {player} إلى اللعبة". Or anchor the verb on a noun of fixed gender: "انضم اللاعب {player}"، or drop the verb: "طلب تبادل من {player}".
- Inserted adjectives can't agree: an adjective follows its noun and matches gender and definiteness, so "{pet} {rarity}" gives "قطة أسطوري" (wrong: أسطورية). Show the rarity as a tag: "{pet} ({rarity})" or "الندرة: {rarity}". Ship rarity labels in the masculine tag form (أسطوري، نادر، ملحمي).
- No article on a param: never "ال{pet}". If the sentence needs "the", rephrase around it.
- One-letter prepositions (بـ، لـ) join the next word. Before a {param} or a Latin word, write them with tatweel and a space, as Roblox does ("خاص بـ Premium"، "لـ Roblox"), or use a full word: "إلى {player}"، "مقابل {price}".
- Word order: verb first (VSO) is natural MSA and keeps every line starting with Arabic: "ينتهي الحدث بعد {time}", not "الحدث ينتهي...".
- Possessives: attach the suffix, don't write "الخاص بك": "حقيبتك ممتلئة", not "الحقيبة الخاصة بك ممتلئة". Drop "your" when obvious: "لكل صديق في الخادم".
- Two verbs, one object: "Buy and equip pets" -> "شراء الحيوانات وتجهيزها", not "شراء وتجهيز الحيوانات".
- Hamza at the start of verbs changes the meaning: أدخل (type in, Form IV) vs ادخل (go into, Form I). "أدخل الكود" but "ادخل الدائرة".
- No capital letters exist: Title Case means nothing. Mark a button or item named inside a sentence with straight quotes: اضغط على "استلام".

## Typography and formatting
- Arabic comma "،", question mark "؟" and semicolon "؛"; of Roblox's 1,594 Arabic strings, none uses a Latin comma and only 2 a Latin "?". Period, "!", ":" and "..." are the same characters as English. No space before, one space after.
- Quotes: straight double quotes "..." (Roblox and Microsoft both); no angle quotes « » and no curly quotes.
- Digits: Western 0-9 only, as Roblox and Microsoft do. Keep game-formatted numbers as given ("1,250"، "1.5"); never convert to ١٢٥٠.
- Percent: ASCII "%" right after the number, no space: "خصم 10%"؛ never "٪" or "% 10". Drop leading + and - signs: in an RTL line "+25%" shows as "25%+". Use a word: "تعزيز 25%"، "زيادة 10%"، "خصم 20%".
- Multiplier: keep the source's "2x" / "{multiplier}x" as one Latin unit, after an Arabic word: "تعزيز دائم 2x".
- Durations: if the game formats {time} per locale, emit Roblox's own Arabic countdown style: number, space, letter unit, with س (hours), د (minutes), ث (seconds) and the word يوم for days: "3 س 20 د"، "2 يوم 5 س" (Roblox's web: "إيقاف البيع بعد {h} س {m} د {s} ث"). Clock style "03:20:15" also works. A Latin "3h 20m" still renders as one LTR run after "بعد", but reads foreign.
- Tanween fath goes before the alef, as in 162 of 170 cases in Roblox's Arabic strings (Microsoft's guide puts it on the alef; follow Roblox): مجانًا، حاليًا، شهريًا. No other diacritics, except shadda where a word is ambiguous or a learner word helps (تحرّك، مشغّل، بِع).
- Never stretch words with tatweel for layout; never use dashes in UI strings, use a period or "،".
- Bidi, the big one: with TextDirection "Auto", Roblox sets each paragraph's direction from its first strong letter. A string that starts with {player}, a Latin word, "VIP ..." or "2x ..." becomes an LTR line: left-aligned, with the name or number on the wrong side. Start every mixed string with an Arabic word: "انضم {player} إلى اللعبة", not "{player} انضم إلى اللعبة".
- Inside an Arabic line, numbers, "1.5x", "50%", "3h 20m", "E" and "Robux" keep their own left-to-right order automatically. Don't reorder them by hand and don't add invisible LRM/RLM marks unless a Studio test shows a bug.
- Keyboard keys stay Latin capitals: "اضغط على E للفتح"، "اضغط على Shift للركض".

## Do not translate / keep as is
- Roblox, Robux, Premium, Roblox Plus: Latin, exactly as Roblox's Arabic UI writes them.
- Game titles stay as the developer ships them (usually Latin "Pet Hatch Tycoon"); put an Arabic word before them in sentences: "مرحبًا بك في Pet Hatch Tycoon!"
- Promo codes (FREEPETS, case and all), display names, keybinds (E, Shift, Space) and VIP stay Latin.
- Item, pet and place names translate ("Lucky Potion" -> "جرعة الحظ"، "Dragon" -> "تنين"), except meme names (Tung Tung Tung Sahur) and names the developer marks as fixed.
- Transliterate only established loans that kids know: الأفاتار، كود، باركور. Otherwise use the Arabic word.
- Arabic script only for Arabic words. Never Arabizi (Latin letters with digits, "3arabi", "7elw"), even though players chat in it.

## Rendering on Roblox
- Shaping and RTL ordering are built in: RtlTextSupport (StarterGui) defaults to Enabled since August 22, 2023, and TextLabels take TextDirection Auto / LeftToRight / RightToLeft. Never reverse Arabic strings by hand (the pre-2023 workaround); reversed text now shows backwards and unjoined.
- Font: the Roblox client ships one Arabic face, Noto Naskh Arabic UI Regular (in the player's content/fonts folder, checked Nov 2026), so Arabic falls back to it whatever Font the label uses (fallback per font unverified). Only the Regular weight ships: next to a heavy display font Arabic headings look thin and small (whether Roblox fakes bold is unverified). Test titles and buttons; a UIStroke helps.
- Naskh has deep descenders and marks above and below (ي ج ع، shadda، tanween); TextScaled labels sized for Latin can shrink or clip them. Check every string at its max length on a phone-size viewport.
- RichText: the Phase 2 notes say text inside a tag gets its own direction but tags are still laid out left to right, so a `<b>` span mid-sentence may put the three pieces in LTR order (unverified since 2023). Test the Lucky Potion banner. If it breaks, wrap the whole string in `<b>...</b>`: one run, same tags, so the tag check still passes. `<font dir="rtl">` is the documented direction override, but it adds a tag the source lacks.
- Typewriter effects: MaxVisibleGraphemes showed nothing for partial Arabic text until a fix on October 29, 2025; retest any typewriter animation.
- Reported RTL bugs: BillboardGui text ordering (reported March 2023, marked fixed June 5, 2023; still worth one look) and legacy chat (switch to TextChatService). Roblox does not mirror your layout: check that Arabic labels sit sensibly in left-aligned frames (alignment flip unverified).

## Mistakes machine translation makes here
- "قم بـ" calque: "قم بشراء البيضة" -> "اشترِ البيضة" (or the button "شراء").
- "الخاص بك" everywhere: "الحقيبة الخاصة بك ممتلئة" -> "حقيبتك ممتلئة".
- Dialect leaking in: "عايز تبادل معاك" / "تبي تبادل؟" -> "يريد التبادل معك".
- Wrong hamza: "أدخل الدائرة" (insert the circle) -> "ادخل الدائرة"; "ادخل الكود" -> "أدخل الكود".
- Ambiguous one-word sources: Roblox's own Arabic has "Back" as "رجوع" (the button) and as "الجزء الخلفي" (the avatar's back slot). Read the context; a navigation button rendered "الجزء الخلفي" is nonsense.
- Number agreement: "2 عملات"، "15 عملات" -> rephrase to "العملات: {count}" (or "عملتان"، "15 عملة" in fixed text).
- Adjective order and agreement: "أسطوري تنين" -> "تنين أسطوري"; "قطة أسطوري" -> "قطة أسطورية".
- Literal gamer slang: "OP" -> "مُبالغ في قوته" -> "خارق"; "Rebirth" -> "البعث" (resurrection) -> "ميلاد جديد".
- Transliterating what Roblox keeps Latin: "روبوكس"، "بريميوم" -> "Robux"، "Premium".
- Eastern digits and Arabic percent: "٥٠٪" -> "50%".
- Outdated platform terms: "جهات التواصل" / "جهات الاتصال" (Connections) -> "الأصدقاء"; "التجربة" -> "اللعبة".
- Stiff vocabulary: "يتسنى لك"، "سوف تحصل"، "عزيزي اللاعب" -> "يمكنك"، "ستحصل"، (cut).
